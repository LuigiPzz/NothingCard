# Nothing Card 🌑💎

A minimalist, premium loyalty card wallet designed with the **Nothing OS** aesthetic. Store all your supermarket, dining, and retail cards in one beautiful, monochrome interface.

## Features ✨
- **Nothing Design Language**: Monochrome palette, Space Mono typography, and dot-matrix elements.
- **Dynamic Search**: Instantly find what you need with a smart placeholder showing your library size.
- **QR & Barcode Support**: Optimized square layout for QR codes (Alice Pizza, etc.) for maximum scanability.
- **200+ Vendors**: Auto-recognition for over 210 Italian and international brands.
- **Cloud Sync**: Securely backup and restore your cards using Google Drive.
- **AMOLED-first**: Deep black backgrounds to save battery and look stunning on OLED screens.

## Tech Stack 🛠️
- **Kotlin & Jetpack Compose**: Modern Android UI toolkit.
- **Hilt**: Dependency injection.
- **Room**: Local database for offline access.
- **ML Kit**: Advanced barcode scanning.
- **ZXing**: High-quality barcode generation.
